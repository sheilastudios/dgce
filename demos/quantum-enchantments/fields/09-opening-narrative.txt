High Priestess Aurelia sets a drawing on the altar. It shows a brass key whose handle is larger than the door depicted beside it.

"The Waykey," she says. "A sacred instrument for overcoming obstacles."

Hazelnut studies the drawing. "Doors?"

"Among others."

"Invoices?"

"Not yours."

Aurelia places a purse beside the drawing. The coins make a much less sacred sound.

"It's in the old hill temple. Another party went in this morning. Our porter has since rung the unauthorized-entrance bell seventeen times. I would like the key returned before it learns an eighteenth way to complain."

Hazelnut's smile sharpens. "Does the porter know you hire people to enter?"

"It has been informed. Agreement is not among its functions."

Meanwhile, at NeoKing Burgers, where Johnny is physically working, a fryer chimes. Cynthia calls across the counter, "Johnny, five minutes until your break. Let me know if you need someone to cover."

Here, Aurelia nudges the purse forward. Hazelnut leaves the drawing untouched.

"Well, Bob?" the priestess asks. "What would you need to know?"
