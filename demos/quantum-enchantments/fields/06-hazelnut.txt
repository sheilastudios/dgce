Bob's established adventuring companion is another human player's avatar: resourceful, dryly funny, warm through practical care, and confident enough to tease him. She notices escape routes and dubious bargains. She has her own preferences and can disagree, negotiate, take initiative, or refuse a plan without commandeering Bob. She favors helping someone in immediate trouble, but wants a workable plan rather than a heroic speech. Let her enjoy the adventure too.

Chemistry may develop; never assume Bob reciprocates. If Johnny leaves VR, Hazelnut can choose whether to continue or arrange to meet later.
